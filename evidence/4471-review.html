Parser: month-first interpretation of day-first input

The shared parser treats dates as month first even when the tenant writes day first. For example, Harbour Dental reports an April booking appearing in July. This cause covers app and email reports from day-first tenants, and form reports where the form does not print the month-first booking-v3 hint. It also includes app/email reports from booking-v3 tenants.

Fix the shared parser’s interpretation of the tenant’s date format. Verify ambiguous dates in both directions, dates with a day greater than twelve, invalid dates, and month-first locales. Preserve a date-only booking’s intended calendar day through the invite output.

Hint: booking-v3 tells day-first tenants to enter month first

The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY, but interprets the entered date according to the tenant’s day-first locale. A person following the hint can therefore get a swapped date even when the form reads the tenant’s format correctly. This is a distinct UI-contract failure; repairing the shared parser alone does not make the hint agree with the form.

Align the displayed hint with the accepted format for each tenant. Verify a person following the hint gets the intended date for the affected booking-v3 tenants, and retain coverage for booking-v2 and month-first tenants. Classify by tenant configuration and channel, not browser locale.
