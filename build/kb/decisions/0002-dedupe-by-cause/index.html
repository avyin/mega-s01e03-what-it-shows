Context

The review of #4471 finds distinct parser and booking-form hint failures in a ticket described as one issue. Subject matching hides that distinction. The old accepted decision 0001 is preserved unchanged.

Proposed replacement

Use the failure mechanism as the duplicate key. Read the body and resolve tenant locale, form version and input channel. Subject and intake tags help search; neither proves a duplicate. Where the supplied classifier cannot place a report, retain it separately and gather missing context.
